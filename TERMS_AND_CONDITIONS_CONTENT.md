## 2. Services Provided
NextGen Tutors provides a platform to match high school learners (Gr 8–12) with qualified tutors in Johannesburg and online nationally.

## 3. Academic Policy
- **Guarantee**: We offer a "First Lesson Free" guarantee on your first matched tutor session. If unsatisfied, we will match you with another tutor at no additional cost for that session.
- **Vetting**: We vet all tutors for ID, police clearance, and academic credentials. However, parents remain responsible for supervising sessions in the home.

## 4. Payment & Billing
- **Direct Payouts**: All payments must go through the NextGen Tutors platform. Paying tutors directly is a breach of these terms.
- **Rates**: Rates are determined by grade level and delivery format (In-person vs Online).
- **Invoices**: Invoices are generated at the start of the month or per-session for ad-hoc bookings.

## 5. Cancellations & Refunds
- **24-Hour Rule**: Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance to receive a full credit.
