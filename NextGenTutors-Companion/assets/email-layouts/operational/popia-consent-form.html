POPIA Data Processing Consent

NextGen Tutors (Pty) Ltd processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). By proceeding, you explicitly consent to:

  • Collection: Name, contact details, grade level, payment information, and session metadata.
  • Purpose: Booking management, tutoring delivery, quality assurance, platform analytics, and payout processing.
  • Lesson delivery: Live online or in-person lessons. Product session recording is not offered. Personal information is retained for service duration then minimised per POPIA.
  • Third-Party Sharing: Secure processing via PayFast/Peach Payments (payments), Zoom (video), and FluentCRM (communications). No data is sold or used for unrelated marketing.

You may withdraw consent or request data access/deletion by emailing privacy@nextgentutors.co.za. Full policy: Privacy Policy.
