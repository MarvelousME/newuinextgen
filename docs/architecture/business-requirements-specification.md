# Business Requirements Specification (BRS)

## Business Objectives

1. Acquire and retain tutoring customers (parents/students).
2. Onboard and activate quality tutors at scale.
3. Maintain trustworthy operations with auditable controls.
4. Enable finance/support/admin efficiency through role-targeted tooling.

## Business Capabilities and Status

| Capability | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Parent acquisition and conversion | end-to-end parent journey to booking | VERIFIED |
| Tutor supply onboarding | application to approval/rejection/resubmission | VERIFIED |
| Revenue operations | invoice/payment/payout lifecycle | PARTIAL |
| Quality assurance | review/rating and vetting controls | VERIFIED |
| Operations governance | verification/self-healing/audit | VERIFIED |
| Data-driven decisions | analytics + attribution + profiling | VERIFIED |

## Stakeholder Requirements

- Business owner: KPI visibility and operational confidence.
- Admin: lifecycle controls and health diagnostics.
- Tutor: application transparency and payout visibility.
- Parent/student: clear booking and session workflows.
- Support: issue triage and escalation paths.
