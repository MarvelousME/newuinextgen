# NextGen Tutors — Privacy Policy (Extracted)

## 1. Information Collection
We collect personal information including names, contact details, ID numbers (for tutors), and academic records to facilitate the matching process.

## 2. Use of Data
Data is used exclusively for:
- Matching learners with tutors.
- Processing payments via secure gateways.
- Improving our educational algorithms.

## 3. POPIA Compliance
NextGen Tutors adheres to the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We do not sell or share your data with third-party marketing firms.

## 4. Academic Data
Learner progress and lesson history are stored securely for parent review and quality control.

## 5. Contact
For data requests or deletion, contact: privacy@nextgentutors.co.za
