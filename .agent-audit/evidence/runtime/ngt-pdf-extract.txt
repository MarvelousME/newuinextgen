Our Story
NextGen Tutors was founded in 2020 by a team of South African educators who saw firsthand
the challenges learners face in accessing quality educational support.
We recognized that traditional tutoring was expensive, inconsistent, and often difficult to
access — especially for families outside major metros. Meanwhile, talented educators
struggled to find flexible work opportunities.
Our platform bridges this gap, connecting verified tutors with learners across South Africa
through a safe, affordable, and effective online platform.
